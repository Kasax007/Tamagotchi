# Tamagotchi – CurseForge description (draft)

**Find them. Tame them. Love them.** Ten rare pets hide in Minecraft's structures – a chubby Desert Dragon in
the desert pyramids, a leafy Jungle Dragon in jungle temples, a Tide Dragon at ocean monuments, a little penguin in
a scarf in igloos, and six more. Tame one with a **Tamagotchi Heart** and it becomes your companion – one that
needs you, just like the little egg on your keychain did.

## Ten pets, ten talents

* **Desert Dragon** – roasts your raw food and smelts raw ore with a puff of flame
* **Jungle Dragon** – makes crops and saplings grow
* **Tide Dragon** – Water Breathing and Dolphin's Grace while you swim together
* **Frost Penguin** – freezes water under your feet, belly-slides on ice
* **Ember Pup** – keeps you safe from fire, swims through lava
* **Sculk Wisp** – a sonar ping makes hostile mobs glow through walls
* **Void Kitten** – pulls dropped items into its backpack
* **Pebble Golem** – sniffs out ores and points the way
* **Hex Toad** – brews you helpful potion effects
* **Mimic** – a living chest with a 54-slot belly that coughs up treasure

They are not guaranteed: each structure has a *chance* to hold its pet, so every find is special.

## A real pet

* **Needs:** hunger, fun, energy and hygiene make up its mood. Happy pets use their talent, sad ones sulk.
* **Tama Pager:** a handheld virtual-pet device. Opens the pet screen and **beeps** when your pet needs you.
* **Backpack:** sneak + right-click with a chest – your pet carries 27 slots for you (and spills everything if you take it off!).
* **Snack pouch:** give it golden apples; it runs over and feeds you one when you are low on health.
* **Fights with you**, but when it gets hurt it retreats to you and eats from its backpack to heal.
* **Never lost:** a tamed pet faints into an egg instead of dying.
* Sit / Follow / Wander, leads, name tags.

## Lots of love (and animations)

Petting, eating, sleeping, dancing to jukebox music, hopping to note blocks, warming up at the campfire, sniffing
flowers, begging for cake, shaking off water, hiding from thunder, chasing a **laser pointer**, fetching a
**squeaky ball**, pouncing on a **feather wand**, playing with other pets – and expressive faces with heart eyes.

## Furniture with a purpose

Pet beds, a wicker basket and a dragon nest to sleep in, a food bowl they eat from by themselves, a bath with a
rubber duck, a bouncy mushroom everyone can jump on, a running wheel that powers redstone, a scratching post and
a little dog house to shelter from the rain.

Requires Fabric API. Minecraft 26.3.
