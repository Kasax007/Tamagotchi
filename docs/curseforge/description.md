**Tamagotchi** brings the little egg on your keychain into Minecraft. **Ten rare pets** hide in the world's structures: a chubby Desert Dragon in the pyramids, a leafy Jungle Dragon in the temples, a Tide Dragon at the ocean monument, a penguin in a scarf in an igloo, and six more. Find one, tame it with a **Tamagotchi Heart**, and it becomes your companion: it follows you, carries a backpack, fights beside you, has a special talent, and, just like the original, it **needs you**. Feed it, play with it, keep it clean and let it sleep, and it will love you back.

Have an idea for a pet, a toy or a piece of furniture? Tell me on [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/zuZVtVvJaY).

![The ten pets](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kasax007/Tamagotchi/26.3/docs/curseforge/img/strip_pets.png)

Every pet lives in its own structure and every structure only *has a chance* to hold it, so each find is something special. Wild pets stay close to home and never despawn. The cute ones run away when hit; the Ember Pup, Sculk Wisp and Mimic bite back. Hold a **Tamagotchi Heart** and they come closer.

![The ten pets, where to find them and their talents](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kasax007/Tamagotchi/26.3/docs/curseforge/img/pets.png)

* **Desert Dragon** (desert pyramid) - *Ember Roast:* roasts raw food and smelts raw ore with a puff of flame
* **Jungle Dragon** (jungle temple) - *Green Thumb:* crops and saplings around it grow
* **Tide Dragon** (ocean monument) - *Tide Blessing:* Water Breathing and Dolphin's Grace while you swim together
* **Frost Penguin** (igloo) - *Frost Steps:* water freezes under your feet
* **Ember Pup** (nether fortress) - *Warm Heart:* Fire Resistance when you catch fire, and it swims in lava
* **Sculk Wisp** (ancient city) - *Echo Sense:* a sonar ping makes hostile mobs glow through walls
* **Void Kitten** (end city) - *Item Magnet:* pulls dropped items into its backpack
* **Pebble Golem** (trail ruins, mineshafts) - *Ore Sniffer:* sniffs out the best ore nearby and makes it glow
* **Hex Toad** (swamp hut) - *Brew Buddy:* brews you helpful potion effects
* **Mimic** (trial chambers, woodland mansion) - *Treasure Belly:* a 27-slot belly (54 with a backpack) that coughs up treasure

![A real pet](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kasax007/Tamagotchi/26.3/docs/curseforge/img/strip_care.png)

A Tamagotchi is not a decoration. Four needs, **Hunger, Fun, Energy and Hygiene**, make up its **mood**, which you can read on the pet screen and as little bubbles over its head. Happy pets use their talent, sad pets sulk, and miserable pets refuse to fight.

* **Hunger:** Pet Kibble, its favourite treat, its usual food, or a Food Bowl it eats from all by itself
* **Fun:** petting, a Laser Pointer, a Squeaky Ball (fetch!), a Feather Wand, a Bouncy Mushroom, a Running Wheel, a Scratching Post, or just other pets
* **Energy:** sleeping in a Pet Bed, Pet Basket or Dragon Nest at night or whenever it is tired
* **Hygiene:** the Pet Brush or a filled Pet Bath

Every bit of care grows your **bond**, shown as hearts on the pet screen. The stronger the bond, the faster talents recharge.

The **Tama Pager** is your handheld device: right-click your pet to make it stay or follow, use it in the air to open your nearest pet's screen, and it **beeps** when a pet is hungry, bored, sleepy, dirty or hurt. You get a free one the first time you tame a pet.

![Backpack and golden apples](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kasax007/Tamagotchi/26.3/docs/curseforge/img/strip_backpack.png)

Sneak + right-click with a **chest** and your pet puts on a **backpack with 27 slots**. It carries your stuff, and its talent may fill it for you. Take it off with shears, but careful: **everything inside spills out!**

Slip a **golden apple** into the backpack and your pet will keep an eye on your health. When it gets low, your pet runs over and **feeds you one**. Best friend, best snack pouch.

![Fights with you](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kasax007/Tamagotchi/26.3/docs/curseforge/img/strip_fights.png)

In a fight your pet helps you like a wolf would. When it gets hurt, it **retreats to you and eats from its backpack** to heal up, so keep some food in there. Tamed pets **never die**: when one runs out of health it **faints into a Tama Egg** that keeps everything: name, stats and backpack. Use the egg to wake your friend up again.

![Dragons can fly](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kasax007/Tamagotchi/26.3/docs/curseforge/img/strip_dragons.png)

The three dragons can **fly**: when it is faster, for example to catch up with you after you sprinted off, or simply when they feel like it.

![Controls cheat sheet](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kasax007/Tamagotchi/26.3/docs/curseforge/img/controls.png)

| Action | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Right-click** (empty hand) | Pet it |
| **Sneak + right-click** (empty hand) | Open the pet screen: backpack, needs, mood, Sit / Follow / Wander |
| **Sneak + right-click** with a chest | Put a backpack on (27 slots) |
| **Sneak + right-click** with shears | Take the backpack off (everything inside spills out!) |
| **Right-click** with the Tama Pager | Sit / Stay or Follow |
| **Use the Tama Pager in the air** | Opens the screen of your nearest pet. The pager beeps when a pet needs you |
| **Right-click** with food or its favourite treat | Feed it |
| **Right-click** with a golden apple | It goes into the pet's backpack. When your health is low, the pet brings you one |
| **Right-click** with the Pet Brush | Groom it |
| **Tamagotchi Heart** | Tames a wild pet. Lead and name tag work as usual |

![Furniture](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kasax007/Tamagotchi/26.3/docs/curseforge/img/strip_furniture.png)

Furniture is more than decoration: **your pets use it on their own.**

* **Pet Bed, Pet Basket, Dragon Nest:** a place to sleep
* **Food Bowl:** fill it and your pets eat from it by themselves
* **Pet Bath:** fill it with water and it keeps everyone clean
* **Bouncy Mushroom:** everyone bounces
* **Running Wheel:** a pet runs inside it, and it gives off a **redstone signal** while it does
* **Scratching Post:** scratch, scratch, scratch
* **Pet House:** shelter from the rain
![Plushies, portraits, toy box, treat dispenser, incubator](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kasax007/Tamagotchi/26.3/docs/screenshots/furniture_update.jpg)

* **Toy Box:** bored pets open it by themselves and play with the toys inside
* **Plushies:** one of every pet. Squeeze them! Lonely pets cuddle them, and a pet sleeps better next to a plush of its own kind
* **Pet Portrait:** click it and it paints your pet, with its name on a little brass plate
* **Treat Dispenser:** fill it with treats, give it a redstone pulse, and your hungriest pet jumps and catches a treat
* **Pet Incubator:** a Tama Egg hatches in thirty seconds, fully healed
* **Pet Flap:** your pets walk through, everyone else bumps into it
* **Egg Lamp:** a glowing night light
* **Colours:** dye the Pet Bed, Pet Basket, Egg Lamp and the portrait backdrop in all sixteen colours

![FAQ](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kasax007/Tamagotchi/26.3/docs/curseforge/img/strip_faq.png)

**Is there a Forge version?**
No. Tamagotchi is a Fabric mod and needs Fabric API.

**Can I use it in my modpack?**
Yes, please do.

**Are the pets guaranteed?**
No. Each structure only has a chance to hold its pet, so explore more structures!

**What happens when my pet is defeated?**
Tamed pets never die. They faint into a Tama Egg that keeps everything, and you can wake them up again.
