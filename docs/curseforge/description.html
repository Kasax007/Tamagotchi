Tamagotchi brings the little egg on your keychain into Minecraft. Ten rare pets hide in the world's structures. Find one, tame it with a Tamagotchi Heart, and it follows you, carries a backpack, fights at your side and has its own talent. Just like the original, it needs you: feed it, play with it, keep it clean and let it sleep.

Have an idea for a pet, a toy or a piece of furniture? Tell me on Discord.

At a glance

  • 10 pets, each with its own look, sounds and talent, each living in its own structure
  • Needs and moods: hunger, fun, energy and hygiene, a bond that grows, and a Tama Pager that beeps
  • A backpack with 27 slots, and golden apples your pet brings you when you are hurt
  • Pets fight at your side and never die: they faint into a Tama Egg
  • The dragons fly, and furniture and toys your pets use all by themselves
  • An in-game guide (the Tamacon), JEI and REI support, everything craftable

New in 1.1: plushies, gadgets and colours

Plushies of all ten pets, a Pet Portrait, a Treat Dispenser, a Pet Incubator, a Toy Box, a Pet Flap, an Egg Lamp and furniture in all sixteen colours. Details under Furniture below.

Every pet lives in its own structure, and every structure only has a chance to hold it, so each find is special. Wild pets stay close to home and never despawn. The cute ones run away when hit; the Ember Pup, Sculk Wisp and Mimic bite back. Hold a Tamagotchi Heart and they come closer.

  • Desert Dragon (desert pyramid): Ember Roast, roasts raw food and smelts raw ore with a puff of flame
  • Jungle Dragon (jungle temple): Green Thumb, crops and saplings around it grow
  • Tide Dragon (ocean monument): Tide Blessing, Water Breathing and Dolphin's Grace while you swim together
  • Frost Penguin (igloo): Frost Steps, water freezes under your feet
  • Ember Pup (nether fortress): Warm Heart, Fire Resistance when you catch fire, and it swims in lava
  • Sculk Wisp (ancient city): Echo Sense, a sonar ping makes hostile mobs glow through walls
  • Void Kitten (end city): Item Magnet, pulls dropped items into its backpack
  • Pebble Golem (trail ruins, mineshafts): Ore Sniffer, sniffs out the best ore nearby and makes it glow
  • Hex Toad (swamp hut): Brew Buddy, brews you helpful potion effects
  • Mimic (trial chambers, woodland mansion): Treasure Belly, a 27-slot belly (54 with a backpack) that coughs up treasure

A Tamagotchi is not a decoration. Four needs, Hunger, Fun, Energy and Hygiene, make up its mood, shown on the pet screen and as little bubbles over its head. Happy pets use their talent, sad pets sulk, and miserable pets refuse to fight.

  • Hunger: Pet Kibble, its favourite treat, its usual food, a Food Bowl it eats from by itself, or a Treat Dispenser
  • Fun: petting, the Laser Pointer, the Squeaky Ball, the Feather Wand, the Bouncy Mushroom, the Running Wheel, the Scratching Post, the Toy Box, plushies and other pets
  • Energy: sleeping in a Pet Bed, Pet Basket or Dragon Nest at night or when it is tired (faster next to a plush of its own kind)
  • Hygiene: the Pet Brush or a filled Pet Bath

Every bit of care grows your bond (the hearts on the pet screen); the stronger the bond, the faster talents recharge.

The Tama Pager: right-click your pet to make it stay or follow, use it in the air to open your nearest pet's screen, and it beeps when a pet is hungry, bored, sleepy, dirty or hurt. You get one for free when you tame your first pet.

Sneak + right-click with a chest and your pet puts on a backpack with 27 slots. It carries your stuff, and its talent may fill it for you. Take it off with shears, but careful: everything inside spills out!

Slip a golden apple into the backpack and your pet keeps an eye on your health. When it gets low, your pet runs over and feeds you one.

In a fight your pet helps you like a wolf would. When it gets hurt, it retreats to you and eats from its backpack to heal, so keep some food in there.

Tamed pets never die: they faint into a Tama Egg that keeps everything (name, needs, bond and backpack). Use the egg on a block to wake your friend up, or put it into a Pet Incubator: thirty seconds later it hatches fully healed.

The Desert, Jungle and Tide Dragon fly: after you when that is faster (you sprinted off, or you are up on a ledge), and now and then just for the joy of it. Dragons never take fall damage, and the Tama Pager keeps them on the ground.

Furniture is more than decoration: your pets use it on their own.

  • Pet Bed, Pet Basket, Dragon Nest: a place to sleep
  • Food Bowl: fill it and your pets eat from it by themselves
  • Pet Bath: fill it with water and it keeps everyone clean
  • Bouncy Mushroom: everyone bounces
  • Running Wheel: a pet runs inside it and it gives off a redstone signal while it does
  • Scratching Post: scratch, scratch, scratch
  • Pet House: shelter from the rain

Everything is craftable; JEI and REI show every recipe.

Pets react to the world around them: they dance to jukeboxes and hop to note blocks, warm up at campfires, sniff flowers, beg for cake and for your food, hide from thunder, shake off water, cheer when you level up, play with each other and sleep next to your bed. The penguin belly-slides on ice.

No need for a wiki: the Tamacon is a craftable guide book in the game (book, pink dye and redstone). It explains every pet, every item, every piece of furniture and how to take care of them.

How to install on a server

  1. Create a new server with BisectHosting.
  2. Select Minecraft 26.3 and Fabric as the server type.
  3. Start the server once so all files generate, then stop it.
  4. Upload Tamagotchi and Fabric API into the mods folder.
  5. Start the server.
  6. Install the same two mods on your client.

The mod must be installed on both client and server, and both must run the same Minecraft version.

Is there a Forge or NeoForge version?
No. Tamagotchi is a Fabric mod and needs Fabric API.

Can I use it in my modpack?
Yes, please do.

Are the pets guaranteed?
No. Each structure only has a chance to hold its pet, so explore more structures!

What happens when my pet is defeated?
Tamed pets never die. They faint into a Tama Egg that keeps everything, and you can wake them up again (or hatch them in a Pet Incubator).
