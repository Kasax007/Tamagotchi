# Tamagotchi 1.2.0+1.21.1

The **Minecraft 1.21.1** version of Tamagotchi 1.2.0 (Fabric). Same pets, furniture and features as 1.2.0 for 26.3:

- Ten rare pets to find in structures, tame with a Tamagotchi Heart and look after: needs, moods, bond, talents
- Toys (Laser Pointer, Squeaky Ball, Feather Wand, Pet Brush), the Tama Pager with its pet screen, the Tamacon guide
- All 25 pieces of furniture: beds, basket, nest, bowl, bath, bouncy mushroom, running wheel, scratching post, house,
  egg lamp, portrait, toy box, pet flap, treat dispenser, incubator and the ten plushies, with dyeable colours
- Everything from 1.2.0 for modpacks: modded food (`c:foods`), modded ores (`c:ores`), look-alike structures from
  other mods and the `tamagotchi:pet_home/...` structure tags, any wooden chest and any shears for the backpack,
  diet item tags, furniture tooltips, recipe-book unlocks, faster mining with the right tool
- All 1.2.0 fixes (sleeping, golden apples, eggs in the void and in fire or explosions, dragons after loading, Void
  Kitten, owners in another dimension, the pet screen and the Tamacon)

Requires Fabric API for 1.21.1. Recipe viewers (JEI, REI) are optional and show the info pages.
